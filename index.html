A focused, more personal way to manage jewellery enquiries.

This is a visual prototype of the Ethima WhatsApp Concierge MVP: one focused journey that turns website, Instagram, QR, referral and direct enquiries into a structured WhatsApp conversation, an appointment link, and a manual stylist handoff.

MVP boundary

Included: one WhatsApp qualification flow, approved product information, one appointment link and manual stylist handoff. Not included: payments, ecommerce, custom CAD, advanced analytics, AI agents, aftercare campaigns or paid advertising automation.

Suggested review conversation

What should we connect first?

The screens are intentionally limited to one affordable workflow. Use them to confirm the message copy, product links, appointment URL and manual handoff process before implementation begins.

01

Lead intake: Which website, Instagram, QR, referral or direct enquiry should start the flow?

02

WhatsApp: Which approved questions, product links and response windows should be included?

03

Human handoff: When should the conversation be handed to a stylist or gemologist?

04

Measurement: Which basic signals should be reviewed — enquiries, replies, appointment links and handoffs?

Prototype status

Ready for workflow validation.

The current screens are a clickable concept with simulated data and interactions. The next build phase can connect the approved journey to a WhatsApp provider, approved product links and one appointment URL.

Note: This prototype does not send messages, take payments or store real customer information yet.
